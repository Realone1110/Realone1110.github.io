Teaching principles

CONTEXT

Make data tangible

Start from a physical or operational decision, then introduce the statistical or computational model needed to reason about it.

PROCESS

Scaffold reasoning

Help students move from formulation to modeling, computation, validation, and interpretation, with support gradually removed as independence grows.

EVIDENCE

Assess more than the final number

Reward assumptions, model choice, validation, interpretation, reproducibility, and technical communication.

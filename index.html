About

My research combines Bayesian inference, machine learning, uncertainty quantification, and first-principles modeling to solve complex engineering problems. I am particularly interested in methods that remain reliable when data are sparse or noisy, model parameters vary, or the governing physics are only partially known.

Research themes

INFER

Bayesian inverse modeling

Recover latent physical quantities and material or process parameters from indirect measurements while quantifying uncertainty.

DISCOVER

Governing physics under uncertainty

Identify interpretable dynamical models when real systems exhibit measurement noise, limited data, and intrinsic variability.

ACCELERATE

Scientific machine learning

Use neural operators and surrogate models to reduce the cost of repeated high-fidelity simulation for design and decision making.

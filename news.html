News & Updates

Research, awards & professional milestones

Updates from my research, publications, awards, conference activities, teaching, and professional service from 2021 through 2026.

2026

August 2026

New review article published in Transport Phenomena

I’m happy to share that our review article, Open datasets and machine learning for two-phase heat transfer: a review following a spatial-temporal taxonomy, has been published in Transport Phenomena. The work brings together perspectives from thermal sciences, data science, and machine learning.

August 2026

Team RAV wins First Place in the NSF Future Manufacturing Data Challenge

I’m thrilled to share that Team RAV from Arizona State University won First Place in the NSF Future Manufacturing Data Challenge organized through Texas A&M University. This was a rewarding opportunity to work on a challenging data-driven manufacturing problem with an excellent team.

August 2026

Team RAV also receives Best Presentation recognition

In addition to winning the competition, our team received the separate Best Presentation Design Award, publicly announced as Best Presentation. I’m especially pleased that both the technical work and the way we communicated it were recognized.

June 25, 2026

Our physics-discovery work is published in Communications Physics

I’m delighted to share the publication of Discovering Governing Physics in the Presence of Uncertainty in Communications Physics. In this work, we develop a stochastic inverse framework for discovering governing equations when observations contain measurement noise and intrinsic parameter variability.

May 2026

Topology-optimization acceleration work appears at IEEE FCCM 2026

This month, our work Accelerating Topology Optimization on AMD Versal AIE-ML Engines appeared in the proceedings of the IEEE International Symposium on Field-Programmable Custom Computing Machines. The study explores hardware acceleration for computationally intensive topology-optimization workflows.

May 2026

Organizing an invited session for INFORMS 2026

I’m pleased to be co-organizing the invited Data Mining cluster session Data-Driven Modeling and Scientific Discovery in Complex Systems with Yuhao Zhong for the 2026 INFORMS Annual Meeting. The session brings together work in machine learning, inverse modeling, uncertainty quantification, dynamical systems, and scientific discovery.

April 2026

Team RAV wins Problem D in the Semiconductor Solutions Challenge

I’m excited to share that Team RAV won Problem D, Machine Learning for Static Voltage Drop Prediction, in the Semiconductor Solutions Challenge at Arizona State University. It was a great opportunity to apply machine learning to a practical semiconductor engineering problem.

ASU showcase · ASU credential

August 2025

CRONet published in Manufacturing Letters

I’m pleased to share that our paper, CRONet: A convolutional recurrent operator approximator network to accelerate topology optimization, has been published in the NAMRC 53 special supplement of Manufacturing Letters. The work develops a scientific machine-learning approach for accelerating iterative topology optimization.
